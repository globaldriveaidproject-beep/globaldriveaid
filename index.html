Our Foundation

A Mission Born
From Experience

Mankind Global was not built in a boardroom. It was built from lived truth.

My name is Tidimalo Konate. I came to Dubai with a dream — to build something meaningful. What I found was a system that had no place for the vulnerable, the displaced, the forgotten.

During my time in detention in Dubai, I saw women with no support, no dignity, no voice. I saw the gap between the world's wealth and its compassion. That experience didn't break me — it became my mission.

Inspired by the life of Paul, Apostle of Christ — who turned his most painful experiences into the most powerful purpose — I chose to build rather than retreat. To create a system where no one is invisible. No one is left behind.

TIDIMALO KONATE
FOUNDER & CEO — MANKIND GLOBAL
